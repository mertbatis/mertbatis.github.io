Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

video-panel ("the app") is a personal tool that runs locally on its owner's computer. It uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to, and read statistics from, YouTube channels that the owner controls.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

  • Permission to upload videos, set thumbnails and update channel descriptions and banners on the connected channel.
  • Read-only YouTube Analytics data for the connected channel (views, watch time, audience retention, viewer countries, subscribers).

Where data is stored

OAuth tokens and analytics data are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the app, are not sold, and are not shared with third parties. The app has no server, no user accounts and no advertising.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party access settings. Deleting the local token files also removes the app's access on that computer.
